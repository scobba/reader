    Add a document

    On iPhone, tap Browse to pick anything you have saved in Files, including PDFs shared from Safari or a journal app.

    Many publishers block direct fetching from a browser. If a URL fails, open it in Safari, Select All → Copy, and use the Paste tab instead — that always works.

    Voice

    Voices marked sends text online are synthesised on the vendor’s servers, so the text of each sentence leaves your device. Every other voice runs entirely on your phone. Apple’s voices, including the Enhanced and Premium downloads, are all on-device.
